### Full Description
```text
Meet TodoCat — the visual task management app that transforms daily productivity into an engaging experience with an animated running companion!

Inspired by the iconic RunCat runners, TodoCat connects your task completion velocity directly to a lively cat that runs faster as you finish your work. When all your goals are met, your cat rests peacefully, giving you instant, delightful feedback on your daily progress.

KEY FEATURES:
• Dynamic Runner Companion: Cat runs faster as tasks are completed; rests when you're all caught up.
• Floating Cat Screen Overlay: Access today's tasks and quick-add over any app.
• Interactive Home Screen Widgets: Monitor goals and check off tasks directly from your home screen.
• Smart Task Management: Priority flags, categories, due dates, and fast search.
• Productivity Analytics: Weekly and monthly completion charts and activity velocity.
• Themes & Customization: Dark, light, and system themes with black and white cat variants.
• Cloud Sync & Offline-First: Works completely offline and synchronizes seamlessly via Google Sign-In or email.
• Privacy Focused: No tracking ads, full data control and deletion anytime.
```
